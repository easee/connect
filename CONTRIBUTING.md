# Contribution Guidelines

Thanks for your interest in contributing! To ensure a collaborative and constructive environment, please follow these guidelines:

---

## 🧠 General guidelines

- Be respectful and open-minded.
- Use issue templates for reporting bugs or feature ideas.
- Keep discussions on-topic and relevant to the issue.
- Duplicate issues may be closed – please search first.

---

## 🛠 Moderation

- This repo is monitored by Easee employees (product managers and engineers).
- Community comments are welcome on all issues.
- We will triage and label issues to prioritize.

---

## ✅ Best practices

- Keep titles short and descriptive.
- Provide as much detail as possible.
- Include logs, screenshots, and reproducible (repro) steps where applicable.
- Indicate urgency when relevant.

---

## 📜 Code of conduct

Please follow our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) to foster a positive space for collaboration.

---

We appreciate your input and look forward to improving the Easee ecosystem together!
