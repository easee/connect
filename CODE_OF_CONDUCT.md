# Code of Conduct

This project and community are governed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/).

---

## 💬 Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

---

## ✅ Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable behaviour

- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information

---

## 📣 Enforcement

Violations may be addressed by moderators. We reserve the right to remove contributions or take action to ensure a healthy community.

Thank you for being part of the Easee community!
